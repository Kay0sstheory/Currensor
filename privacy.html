Privacy Policy

Currensor · Last updated 3 October 2026

Currensor is a free, open-source currency converter, available as a web app and as a plugin for AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude. It is built to collect as little as possible.

What the plugin receives

When an assistant uses Currensor, it sends only the conversion request: the amount and the currency codes. Currensor does not receive your name, account, email, conversation, or files.

What is stored

Currensor keeps anonymous daily counts: the date, which assistant made the request (for example ChatGPT or Claude), the type of request, and the currency pair (for example CAD to INR). It does not store amounts, IP addresses, or any identifier that could link a request to a person.

Our host, Cloudflare, may keep standard short-lived request logs to run and protect its network, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The web app

The web app runs entirely in your browser. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage, and nothing else is sent to us.

Third parties

Exchange rates come from public rate services (open.er-api.com, with currency-api as a backup). Currensor requests rates from them; your data is not shared with them. We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone.

Children

Currensor is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Kay0sstheory/Currensor/issues.
